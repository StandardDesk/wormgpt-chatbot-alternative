## ✨ About

**WormGPT Chatbot Alternative** is a fully customizable, offline-capable AI chatbot framework designed for developers, researchers, and enthusiasts who want full control over their conversational agents.

It provides a clean, modular architecture that lets you plug in **any LLM backend**, swap **prompt templates**, and build **custom personas** — all while keeping your data private and local.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** This project is intended for **educational and research purposes only**. Always use AI responsibly and in compliance with applicable laws and platform policies.

## 🧩 Project Structure

```
🐛 wormgpt-chatbot-alternative/
├── 🚀 main.py                 # Entry point
├── ⚙️  config.example.yaml     # Sample config
├── 🔌 backends/               # Model adapters
│   ├── openai_backend.py
│   ├── llama_backend.py
│   └── local_backend.py
├── 🎭 personas/               # Bot personalities
├── 🗂️  memory/                 # Vector store & history
├── 🌐 web/                    # Front-end UI
```
